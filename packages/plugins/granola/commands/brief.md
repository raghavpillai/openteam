---
description: Synthesize meeting evidence into a concise briefing on a topic.
---

Read [Granola workflow conventions](../OPENTEAM.md). Prepare a briefing on
$ARGUMENTS for the audience and purpose in the user's request. Use
`granola-context` to find and read relevant notes; use `granola-prep` when the
brief is for an upcoming meeting.

Explain the current position, how it developed, key decisions, involved people,
outstanding commitments, and unresolved questions. Include dates and source
links so the reader can verify the account. Separate current agreements from
older proposals. End with the decisions or questions relevant to the reader's
next conversation. Keep the brief proportionate to the request and disclose
material search or access gaps.
