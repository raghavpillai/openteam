# Spending Limits That May Be Too High

Use this review to find funds whose periodic limits substantially exceed recent cleared
spend. A high limit is not a mistake when spending is seasonal, project-based, or
irregular.

## Investigation

1. Use at least three complete representative months from the shared scan for each
   candidate fund.
2. Rank funds from the scalable fund catalog and shared activity by the apparent gap
   between their listed periodic limit and highest cleared monthly spend. Shortlist a fund
   only when the listed limit is more than twice that peak.
3. Read live fund details for no more than the ten strongest candidates needed for the
   five-result output. Verify the current limit, cadence, owner, members, restrictions,
   pending activity, and any recent changes.
4. Exclude or downgrade candidates with seasonal or irregular spending, annual renewals,
   incomplete history, or upcoming approved work that changes expected spend. The admin chooses the amount for seasonal or irregular spend when a standard target would not fit.

## Recommendation

Suggest the highest month plus 20% as a starting target.
For seasonal or irregular spending, the admin chooses the amount instead of the agent
guessing.

## Safe Change

Show the exact current and proposed positive periodic limit, cadence, affected members,
and potential effect on future purchases. Lower a limit only after explicit confirmation.
Change a limit only when the current connection and permission confirm that action is
available. Immediately verify the updated value with a fresh fund read. Do not suggest
setting a limit to zero or deleting the fund.

## Recommended Output

```text
Finding X: <fund name> may have an oversized limit.
<State the current amount and cadence, highest representative monthly spend, months
checked, and a lower target subject to owner confirmation.>
Next step: Confirm the target before changing the limit.
```
