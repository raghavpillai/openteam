# Create and manage bots

A bot is an AI teammate with its own conversation, memory, and screen to work on. Start with one bot, then add more when you have work that suits different roles.

## Create a bot

On desktop, choose **New Bot** (the **+** at the top of the sidebar). In **Create New Bot**, enter a name, pick a character shape and color, and choose **Create**. On iPhone, tap **+**, then **New Bot**.

A new bot greets you and asks what kind of work you want it to do. Reply with its job and how you like to work, or skip the greeting and send that first:

> You're my documentation assistant. Keep explanations short, check claims against the source, and ask me when a requirement is unclear. Start by reviewing the README I attach next.

The bot remembers this for future tasks. See [memory](../usage/memory.md). For rules it should follow on every task, put them in its **Instructions** instead, as described below.

## Give it a task

A good request says what finished work looks like. Include the files or links it needs and any limits:

> Turn these meeting notes into a decision log. Include the decision, owner, and next step. Mark unknown owners instead of guessing, and attach a Markdown file.

If the task needs one of your accounts, such as Gmail or GitHub, [connect a plugin](../usage/plugins.md). Signing in to a model provider doesn't connect your other accounts.

## Follow up

Keep working in the same conversation. Tell the bot what to change and what to keep. For bigger jobs, ask it to check in with you before it makes changes you can't easily undo.

You can message a bot while it's working. It reads your message right away, so you can add details or change direction mid-task. Routines run separately and keep going while you chat.

## Change a bot's profile

Open the bot and select its name at the top of the conversation to open its settings. You can change its avatar, name, label, description, and **Instructions**.

**Instructions** are standing rules the bot follows on every task, such as how to format reports or what to check before it finishes. They can be up to 20,000 characters.

On desktop, changes save automatically. On iPhone, tap **Instructions** to edit the description and instructions, then go back and tap **Save**.

To turn a bot's notifications on or off, use **Settings → Bot notifications** on iPhone.

## Organize your sidebar

On desktop, right-click a bot in the sidebar to **Pin**, **Move to** a section, **Rename Bot**, **Edit Profile**, or **Delete** it. On iPhone, touch and hold a conversation to **Pin** it, **Move to** a section, or **Delete bot**.

Give bots names that describe their role, such as Research, Operations, or Docs. This makes them easier to tell apart in the sidebar and in group chats.
