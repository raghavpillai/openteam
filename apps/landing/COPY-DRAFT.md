# OpenTeam homepage copy draft

For review. Revised with Claude Fable 5 through Claude CLI and edited against
the product scope in COPY.md. Each section explains a feature and its benefit

## Hero

### Run your own AI team.

Digital workers that run on **your compute** and work in your apps. They have
their **own computer and shared workspace**, remember your instructions, and
**delegate work** to each other.

## Build the team you need

Create specialized workers for the jobs you want to delegate. Give each one a
role and its own instructions, then put your team to work on your own server.

## A computer of their own

Each worker has its own desktop and browser, with a shared workspace for files
they use together. Give them access to your computer when a job needs local
files or tools.

## Bring your apps and data

Give workers the context in your email, calendar, documents and meeting notes,
alongside web search. Connect apps from the plugin marketplace, save workflows
as reusable skills, or add your own tools through MCP.

## They remember how you work

Each worker remembers your instructions, preferences, and past work, so you
spend less time repeating context. They keep their own memory and can draw on
shared knowledge, all stored on compute you control.

## Leave recurring work to your team

Set the instructions once and choose when they run. A worker, or a group of
them, handles the recurring work on schedule and delivers the result without
a reminder from you.

## Workers that help each other

Workers can share context and delegate parts of a job to each other or
specialized helpers. Bring them into a group conversation to work together,
each with its own instructions and memory.

## You stay in control

Choose what workers can do on their own and when they should ask you. Approve
a step once or set rules for future work, and provide sensitive information
privately.

## Take your team with you

The desktop and iPhone apps connect to the same workers, conversations, and
files, so you can pick up work wherever you are.

## Use the model you prefer

Sign in with ChatGPT or Claude, bring your own API keys, or connect a compatible
hosted or local model.

## One command to get started

Run the installer on a machine with Docker. Guided setup configures your server
and model connection.

```sh
curl -fsSL https://openteam.so/install | sh
```
