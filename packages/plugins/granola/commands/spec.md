description: Draft a specification grounded in meeting requirements and decisions.

Read [Granola workflow conventions](../OPENTEAM.md). Draft a specification for
$ARGUMENTS. Search relevant meetings and retrieve notes using `granola-context`.
Use repository or document context to understand the current behavior.

State the problem, intended users, agreed scope, constraints, and expected
behavior. Give requirements stable local identifiers and attach meeting
references to each substantive requirement. Include acceptance criteria,
dependencies, and open questions. Label your proposed details explicitly and
keep them separate from recorded agreements. Identify superseded requirements
when they explain a change. Produce a reviewable draft without publishing it or
inventing agreement where meetings conflict.
